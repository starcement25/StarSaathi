“ Star Cement brings all new mobile application – STAR SAATHI to strengthen relationship with its channel partners. It provides a platform to the Dealers to place order, track order, view ledger and their performance from a mobile device.

The Dealers can place the order for all products available at their location. They will be able to track both online (Order placed through App) and offline (Order placed through phone or mail) in their app. The Outstanding and Ledger up to last fifteen entries will be available. They will be able to view their performance and compare their present sales with previous year’s sale.”
